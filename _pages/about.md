I am Khanh Nguyen, an MSc Philosophy student at the University of Glasgow. I completed my philosophy undergrad (MA Hons Philosophy) at the University of Edinburgh in 2026 with First-Class Honours. My primary research interests are in political realism, political legitimacy, political obligations, liberalism, and social epistemology. I also have a strong background in logic, philosophy of science, general epistemology, and mathematics. On this page, you can find my CV as well as my blog posts.
